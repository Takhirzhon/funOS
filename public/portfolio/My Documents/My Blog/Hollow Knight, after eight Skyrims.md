# Hollow Knight, after eight Skyrims

![The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim](/portfolio/My%20Documents/My%20Blog/Pictures/Skyrim,%20cover.png)

I found a second game I want to come back to. Work is not letting me enjoy it yet - maybe early March, maybe the end of it - but it is on the list.

For years there was only one. The game I could return to, the one that was food and water, where I forgot everything and sank into a beautiful, difficult, strange world, was *The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim*. I first played it in 2017 and have finished it more than eight times since. The last run was about five months ago, and I decided that was enough: better to wait for the sixth one. But there are evenings when you just want to sit and be at peace, and the Skyrim soundtrack over that landscape gave me exactly that.

![Hollow Knight](/portfolio/My%20Documents/My%20Blog/Pictures/Hollow%20Knight,%20cover.jpg)

Then I ran into *Hollow Knight*.

I love it now, but we had a love-hate relationship from August of last year. I installed and uninstalled it four times, because I could not beat the False Knight (died once) and then Hornet (died three times). In December, under a lot of stress from things at home, I gave the game a fifth chance - and beat Hornet on the second try. I was stunned.

After that I played the whole thing, 21 hours, exploring and taking my time. There were moments when I was properly tilted; I would close the game and go work, to get out of it. Playing a little at a time, I finished in mid-January.

Great feelings. But the main thing is that I got lessons out of it. Patience and persistence through trial and error. Faster reactions - my heart pounded the way it does in a sweaty Mobile Legends match (with the usual teammates the matchmaking gives you). And the big one: you do not always solve a new problem by walking straight at it. You are going to hit it, sure - but not always head-on and not only with brute force. Use your head. My boxing coach used to say the same thing about fights: the smartest move is usually not to have one. Some things are not solved alone, either. The game is really about planning your actions and accounting for what might go wrong by studying the world first.

I will be back. It is a top game.
